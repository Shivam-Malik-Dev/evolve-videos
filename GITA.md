## Plan
- 1 shloka per day (2 when the two belong together, e.g. lists of warriors in Chapter 1 — group up to 3 there to keep it interesting).
- Chapter 1 from shloka 1. Chapter 1 is mostly names of warriors: tell it as a story (the setting, Dhritarashtra's question,
  Arjun's breakdown) — keep it gripping.
- Each episode: own curiosity hook → Sanskrit shloka card (mood verse) → word meanings → simple Hindi meaning →
  real-life example for today's viewer → lesson ("Evolve": inner change) → CTA teasing tomorrow's shloka + follow/comment/share.
- Get the Sanskrit text from a reliable source (e.g. Gita Press / holy-bhagavad-gita.org / gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in) and
  cross-check spelling every time. Explain respectfully; mention when a meaning has more than one traditional interpretation.
